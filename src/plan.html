the monetization guardrails (from frame 9f)

  • Seeing what your person drew is never behind the paywall. Not once, not blurred, not delayed.
  • The Thread is not for sale. No streak freezes to buy, nothing to lose by being ill or sad.
  • No guilt notifications, free or paid. Every buzz is a person.
  • One subscription covers both people. The app never asks the second phone for money.
  • Leaving is free. Close the door exports your canvas history at no charge, no win-back emails.
  • If it earns only because leaving hurts, it doesn't earn.

Pricing referenced against the category: Noteit ≈ $6/yr (widget-only), Paired ≈ $9/mo (content-heavy), Widgetable freemium. Trace Plus sits between: cheaper than Paired, far more app than Noteit. The 14-day trial is long on purpose — the product sells itself the first time a drawing arrives at 2am.

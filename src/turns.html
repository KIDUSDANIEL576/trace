9fThe guardrail. The pull has to be the other person, never the app. The list of things we will not build, written down before anyone asks for them.
what we won't do
✕
No breakable streak. The thread goes thin, never snaps. Nothing is lost by being ill, busy, or sad.
✕
No "you haven't drawn in 3 days". We never guilt one person about another. Not once.
✕
No read receipts as leverage. You can see she's here now. You can't audit whether she looked.
✕
No scores, leagues or "relationship health". Two people are not a dashboard.
✕
No notification sent on our own behalf. Every buzz is a person, or it doesn't happen.
✓
You can close the door. One tap ends it, archives the canvas, stops every trace. No "are you sure", no win-back email, no reactivation streak.
If it works only because leaving hurts, it doesn't work.

8eThe brief. Six fields, and the five rules that stop any of these reading as an ad. Hand this to anyone shooting one.
the brief
MOMENT
One thing that actually happened to two real people, on one day, at one time. Name the hour.
NOTIFICATION
The exact words on the lock screen. Six words maximum. It never says what was drawn.
HE DRAWS
One mark. Crude. Two seconds of effort, not twenty.
SHE ANSWERS
The reply is the film. If the reply isn't better than the first drawing, pick another moment.
SOUND
Room tone until the reply lands. Music, if any, enters on the answer — never on the hook.
LENGTH
9–13s. If it needs 20, the moment is too complicated.
FIVE RULES
1 · Nobody speaks. Not one word of dialogue, ever.
2 · Both drawings must be bad. A good drawing makes it an art app.
3 · No faces above the wrist. Hands, phones, rooms.
4 · End on the reply, not the app. The wordmark gets the last half second or nothing.
5 · No UI that isn't in the build. If we haven't shipped it, we don't film it.
If you can tell it's an ad by second three, we shot it wrong.

trace — privacy policy (draft for counsel review)

The short version: trace is for exactly two people. We designed it so we cannot read what matters.

  • Drawings and handwriting are rendered on your devices and exchanged
  • between them. With "On-device only" enabled (default), stroke data is
  • relayed, not stored: our server never keeps a copy after delivery.
  • The pocket exists only on the device that created it. It is excluded
  • from search, sync, backup and export. We could not produce it if asked.
  • Health ("Doctor's note") is never summarized, trended, or synced
  • beyond the question list you wrote. Details are yours to share, not ours.
  • Location is never collected. "Leaving now" shares a minutes-away
  • number your device computes; presence is a dot, never a place.
  • Analytics are counts only (feature opened, stroke sent), never
  • content, tied to a random install id, never sold, never shared.
  • Account: there is none. Your pairing key lives in your platform
  • keychain. Delete everything in Quiet & private → it is gone; there is no
  • server copy of your canvas to delete.
  • Unpairing seals the shared canvas on both devices and destroys the
  • pocket unread. Each person keeps only what was already on their phone.
  • Data we do hold: the pairing relation (two anonymous ids), push tokens,
  • subscription state. Retained while the pairing exists, deleted 30 days
  • after unpairing.

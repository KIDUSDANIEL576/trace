APP NAME (30 max)
Trace: Draw With Your Person

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 max)
Draw on your person's phone. Live. It lands on their home screen.

FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 max)
Trace is a drawing app for two.

Whatever one of you draws appears on the other's phone in real time — stroke
by stroke, as your finger moves. No sending. No waiting. You just draw, and
it's already there.

And then it stays. The Trace widget puts your latest drawing on their home
screen, so they see you every time they pick up their phone. Tap the widget
and their drawing opens full screen — then one more tap and you're drawing
back on it.

WHAT YOU GET

• Draw together, live — one shared canvas, both of you at once, with a
  "…is drawing" pill the moment they start.
• Your page and their page — your own space they can watch fill in, plus a
  common canvas for drawing on the same page together.
• Send a heartbeat — one tap blooms a heart on both screens. Press at the
  same moment and the canvas erupts.
• Draw on your photos — circle their face, annotate the night you remember.
• Replay your story — scrub back through every stroke you've ever drawn.
• Time capsules — seal a drawing until a date. Neither of you can peek.
• 61 skies — dusk, sunset, candlelight, ocean, snow — with a strength slider,
  behind a canvas that stays flat and clean so your ink is the only mark.

PRIVACY IS THE PRODUCT
No feed. No followers. No strangers. No ads. Your drawings are between the
two of you, and deleting your account really deletes everything.

TRACE FOREVER
Free forever: the shared canvas, both personal pages, the widget, a photo a
day, heartbeats. One optional $29.99 purchase unlocks every sky and brush,
unlimited photos and capsules, and full replay — for BOTH of you. One
payment, no subscription.

DESCRIPTION (4000 max)
Trace is a drawing app for two.

Whatever one of you draws appears on the other's phone in real time — stroke
by stroke, as your finger moves. No sending. No waiting. You just draw, and
it's already there.

And then it stays. The Trace widget puts your latest drawing on their home
screen, so they see you every time they pick up their phone.

FIVE THINGS, POLISHED

• Draw together, live
  One shared canvas. Both of you can draw on it at once, and you'll see
  "…is drawing" the moment they start.

• Your page and their page
  Want your own space? You get a page only you draw on — they watch it fill
  in — and they get one too. Plus a common canvas for when you want to play
  on the same page.

• Send a heartbeat
  One tap sends a heart blooming across both screens. Press at the same
  moment and the whole canvas erupts.

• Draw on your photos
  Circle their face. Add a note to a photo from that night. Draw on the
  moments you already have.

• Replay your story
  Every stroke you've ever drawn is kept in order. Scrub back through months
  of little drawings and watch them appear again.

• Time capsules
  Seal a drawing until a date — a week, a year, five years. Neither of you
  can see it until then. Not even you.

A CANVAS THAT GETS OUT OF THE WAY
Pick from 61 skies — dusk, sunset, candlelight, ocean, snow — and dial the
strength until it's exactly as loud as you want behind your ink. The canvas
stays flat and clean, so the only marks on it are yours.

PRIVACY IS THE PRODUCT
Your drawings are between the two of you. No feed, no followers, no
strangers, no ads. Delete your account and it's all gone — completely.
